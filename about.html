How It Started

From problem-solver to skin concierge

Hi, I'm Natasha (Marie) — a full-time working boy mom, Navy Veteran, Lean Six Sigma Black Belt practitioner in supply chain management, and a lifelong skincare lover. After years of personal experience in skincare and a career built on problem-solving, I decided to attend esthetics school to serve others with intention, responsibility, and care.

My esthetics philosophy follows the 80/20 rule: 80% of your skin results come from your home routine, and 20% comes from professional treatments. I research ingredients and products carefully to match each client's unique skin needs — no matter how expensive a product is, you will never get the results you're looking for if it isn't right for your skin.

My goal is long-term, personalized results that last. As a working mom myself, I understand the need for simplicity and flexibility — that's why I offer virtual consultations to make skincare accessible from anywhere.

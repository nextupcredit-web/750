Booking & Cancellation Policy

All appointments must be canceled or rescheduled at least 12 hours in advance. Cancellations or changes within 12 hours forfeit your deposit; no-shows incur a fee. By booking, you agree to this policy. Clients currently taking Accutane (isotretinoin), or who stopped less than 12 months ago, should not receive facial treatments without clearance from their prescribing medical provider.

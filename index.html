The Founder

Meet Natasha Barjon

Hi, I'm Natasha — a full-time working boy mom, Navy Veteran, Lean Six Sigma Black Belt practitioner in supply chain management, and a lifelong skincare lover. After years of personal experience in skincare and a career built on problem-solving, I decided to attend esthetics school to serve others with intention, responsibility, and care.

My philosophy follows the 80/20 rule: 80% of your skin results come from your home routine, and 20% comes from professional treatments — so I research ingredients carefully to match each client's unique skin needs.

How A Visit Works

What to expect at 757esthetics

01

Intake & Consultation

Your visit begins with a thoughtful consultation where Natasha reviews your intake form, listens to your concerns, and learns about your goals, lifestyle, and current routine.

02

Skin Analysis & Facial Assessment

A detailed skin analysis evaluates your skin type, conditions, and sensitivities. During your facial, Natasha observes how your skin responds in real time to exfoliation, hydration, and touch.

03

Customized Treatment Plan

A personalized plan is created — recommended services, ideal frequency, and homecare guidance — plus how future sessions build progressive, long-term results.

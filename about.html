How It Started

From a passion for skin to a studio of her own

Natasha's path into esthetics started the way it does for a lot of people in this industry — with her own skin. Years of trying products that overpromised and underdelivered led her to esthetics school, where a genuine obsession with the science of skin took over.

She trained under working estheticians, studied advanced modalities in facials, peels, and lash artistry, and set out to build something Hampton Roads didn't quite have yet: a studio where every treatment plan starts with an honest conversation, not a sales pitch.

Today, 757 Esthetics is that studio — a calm, welcoming space where clients come back not just for the results, but for the relationship.
